02 / CALL AND RESPONSE

Your phrase.
Frog’s turn.

Choose 4 or 8 bars. The answer lands on the next downbeat and stays in your register. Copy my rhythm can mirror timing, note lengths, and dynamics; rhythm mode can generate up to 8 chords.

  • ✓On the barFollows Live’s bar messages, with a BPM clock as backup.
  • ✓Near your handsVoicing selection keeps the frog in your played register.
  • ✓Your rhythm, optionallyRe-strikes, held lengths, and velocities can shape the answer.

06 / GET IN THE ROOM

Bring a Mac.
Bring a progression.

Jam Frog is a desktop overlay for Ableton Live 12 Suite. The Max for Live devices carry notes and Live’s bar clock between the set and the app.

Open the project ↗
TERMINAL / LOCAL

$ cd app

$ npm install

$ npm run dev

  1. Add Frog Ear to your playing track.
  2. Add Frog Voice to the Frog track.
  3. Choose Max for Live in Jam Frog, then play.
